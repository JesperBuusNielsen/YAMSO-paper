\subsection{Secret Sharing with Robust Reconstruction}
\label{sec:secret-sharing}

For $n$ recipients and privacy threshold $0\le t<n$, let $\mathcal M$ and
$\mathcal S$ be the secret and share spaces.  On secret $s\in\mathcal M$,
$\Share(s)$ samples $(s_1,\ldots,s_n)\in\mathcal S^n$.
Given a vector in $(\mathcal S\cup\{\bot\})^n$, where $\bot$ denotes a
missing share, $\Reconstruct$ deterministically returns a secret or $\bot$.
We use Shamir sharing with error-and-erasure reconstruction~\cite{Shamir1979SecretSharing,McElieceSarwate1981SecretSharing}.
Any $t+1$ correct shares suffice when the rest are missing.  For
$n\geq3t+1$, reconstruction also recovers the secret when up to $t$
positions in total are incorrect or missing.  String secrets are encoded
as field vectors and shared componentwise with independent randomness.

In Shamir sharing any $t$ shares are jointly uniform, independently of
the secret. Suppose the shares at positions $I$, with $|I|\le t$, have
already been exposed as $(z_i)_{i\in I}$. For a target secret $s$, the call
$\CompleteShares(I,(z_i)_{i\in I},s)$ samples exactly the distribution
of $\Share(s)$ conditioned on $s_i=z_i$ for $i\in I$.
It can also return the corresponding sharing coins.
This property is called \emph{resampleability}~\cite[Definition~2.6 and Remark~2.2]{HazayEtAl2025Resampling}.
Appendix~\ref{app:secret-sharing} gives the privacy experiment and the
interpolation proof.
