\subsection{IND-CPA Public-Key Encryption}
\label{sec:ind-cpa}

State transfer uses a public-key encryption scheme
$\PKE=(\KeyGen,\Enc,\Dec)$ with the syntax and correctness of
Section~\ref{sec:nce} and deterministic decryption. Each round circuit
holds the secret key for its incoming state. IND-CPA security means that
even an adversary choosing two equal-length messages after seeing the
public key cannot distinguish their encryptions with non-negligible
advantage~\cite{GoldwasserMicali1982Encryption,GoldwasserMicali1984Encryption,BellareEtAl1998EncryptionNotions}.
Appendix~\ref{app:ind-cpa} gives the experiment.
